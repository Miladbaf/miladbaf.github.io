About

I am a PhD student at Koç University in Istanbul working on AI applications in wireless communications, advised by Professor Sinem Coleri (IEEE Fellow). I graduated with a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Sabancı University (with high honors), with a minor in Physics.

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, deep learning, and wireless communications. I have contributed to research on LLM agents, mechanistic interpretability, and FET modeling, resulting in publications at venues including ICML, IEEE SMACD, and IEEE OJCOMS.

Before returning to academia, I spent several years as an AI product manager, leading teams that shipped AI-driven products to tens of thousands of users. That combination of research depth and industry experience lets me bridge theoretical advances with practical systems.

Sec. III

Research

Sep 2025 — Jan 2026 Sabancı University

AI for Wireless Communications

Developed CORTEX, a transformer-based beam selection system for terahertz (THz) UAV communications. The architecture combines convolutional front-ends with causal transformers using rotary position embeddings (RoPE) for drone state prediction. Key techniques include a two-stage approach for angle prediction and beam selection, circular-aware angle refinement, knowledge distillation, and focal loss for class imbalance.

Supervised by Prof. Özgür Gürbüz

Accepted to IEEE OJCOMS · Q1, JIF 6.1
Sep 2025 — Dec 2025 Sabancı University

Backdoor Attacks on Time-Series Models

Security evaluation and attack development for state-of-the-art time-series models. Implemented and extended two recent approaches: input-aware poisoning attacks that craft sample-specific triggers, and generative-model-based trigger synthesis (GAN / conditional generative models) to produce stealthy backdoors against RNNs, LSTMs, and transformer-based time-series classifiers. Conducted threat-model design, empirical evaluations on standard benchmarks, and initial defenses.

Supervised by Prof. Emre Özfatura

Jan 2025 — Mar 2025 Shanghai AI Lab · remote

Mechanistic Analysis of Language Models

Researched mechanistic analysis and interpretability of transformer-based language models to enhance AI reliability and safety. Implemented a Meta-CoT-based approach to automate mechanistic interpretability in transformer architectures.

Supervised by Wenyu Du (HKU) and Dr. Jie Fu (Shanghai AI Lab)

Feb 2024 — Jun 2025 Sabancı University · graduation project

FET Modeling Using Deep Learning

Developed robust FET models using data-driven techniques, exploring data augmentation strategies including GANs and autoencoders. Enhanced a limited dataset of 80,000 samples from 27 transistors for improved semiconductor device modeling.

Supervised by Prof. Korkut Kaan Tokgöz

Oral presentation · IEEE SMACD 2025
Oct 2023 — May 2024 Yale University · remote

LLM Agent Framework for Strategic Decision-Making

Conducted research on enhancing decision-making by LLMs in multi-agent settings by integrating external tools. Developed the STRIDE framework for strategic and interactive decision-making.

Supervised by Prof. Zhuoran Yang and Dr. Chuanhao Li

AutoRL Workshop · ICML 2024 INFORMS Workshop · EC 2024
Jul 2023 — Oct 2023 Sabancı University

Political Affiliation Analysis Using CV and ML

Implemented self-supervised clustering algorithms (SwAV and Barlow Twins) to categorize over 7 million Twitter profile pictures, estimating the distribution of political affiliations and human/bot ratios.

Supervised by Prof. Onur Varol
